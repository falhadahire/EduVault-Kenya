    Partner assessor & employer verification

    TVET instructors, NGO assessors and employers can look up a Reference ID. Try EV-2026-4109.

    Partner & scale model

    • UNHCR and DRS identify learners and confirm refugee status.
    • ILO PROSPECTS sets the skills-validation approach and funds cohort rollout.
    • TVET centres and NGO assessors run practical assessments and bridging courses.
    • KNQA and statutory bodies confirm equivalency and licensing.
    • Employers verify a Reference ID before hiring.

    Each cohort reuses the same record: map, bridge, assess, certify, place. Prototype scoring is simulated for demonstration and is not an official KNQA decision.
