## 📋 Overview
**EduVault Kenya** is a lightweight, responsive web application designed to help refugees and displaced learners in Kenya (including Dadaab, Kakuma, Nairobi, and Garissa) translate, benchmark, and verify foreign academic diplomas, vocational/TVET certificates, and hands-on trade portfolios against Kenyan National Qualifications Authority (**KNQA**) standards.

---

## ✨ Key Features
* **Credential Equivalency Mapper:** Benchmarks foreign degrees, diplomas, and vocational trades (*Nursing & Healthcare*, *Primary/Secondary Education*, *Solar & Electrical Engineering*, *Accounting & Business*, and *Hospitality & Tailoring*) to Kenyan KNQA levels and generates a unique Reference ID (e.g., `EV-2026-4109`).
* **3-Step Bridging Course Tracker:** Guides candidates through targeted bridging milestones (*Enrol*, *Practical Assessment*, *Certified*) to close qualification gaps.
* **Work & Statutory Compliance Checklist:** Tracks DRS Refugee ID, KRA PIN registration, fee-exempt Class M Work Permit readiness (Kenya Refugees Act 2021), statutory regulatory body clearance (Nursing Council, TSC, NITA), and M-Pesa/bank payout readiness.
* **Partner Assessor & Employer Verification Portal:** Enables local TVET instructors, NGO assessors, and hiring partners to look up Reference IDs, co-sign practical evaluations, and match candidates to local or remote roles.
* **One-Click Sample Cases:** Pre-loaded demonstration profiles representing displaced professionals across Kenya for rapid testing and evaluation.

## 🌍 UN SDG Alignment
* **SDG 4 (Quality Education):** Supports recognition of prior learning, foreign credential equivalency, and targeted TVET bridging.
* **SDG 8 (Decent Work & Economic Growth):** Connects verified refugee professionals and artisans with formal and remote employment.
* **SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities):** Removes systemic credential and licensing barriers facing displaced talent.
* **SDG 16 & 17 (Strong Institutions & Partnerships):** Builds transparent verification rails uniting UNHCR, ILO, local TVETs, and hiring partners.
